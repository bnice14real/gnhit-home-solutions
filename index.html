Switching, Wi-Fi, cabling, and someone who still knows the password list.

A network is not a router from a big-box shelf. We survey the site, pull or tidy cable, place access points where people actually sit, and separate staff, guests, cameras, and point-of-sale. Then we stay on as the administrator.

  • Switching, VLANs, and Wi-Fi that covers the building

Firewall installation & configuration

The appliance, the rules, the VPN, and a log someone reads.

We install or replace the firewall, then write rules that match how the site works. Guest Wi-Fi does not touch the office. Remote staff get a VPN. Logging feeds the night desk. We work with Fortinet, SonicWall, Ubiquiti, and the box you already own when it is still the right box.

  • Install, swap, and harden

  • Site-to-site and remote-user VPN
  • Change notes, not a mystery config

What the night shift actually does.

You should know who is awake. This is the shift, not a slogan.

Watch

Firewall drops, VPN failures, server and cloud alerts, backup jobs that did not finish, recorders that stop talking, disks that are filling up.

Confirm

We check whether it is a flap or a real outage before we wake you. Noise stays on the desk. A dark shop, a dead till network, or a recorder that stopped does not.

Act

We follow the runbook you signed: restart, fail over, call you, or call the carrier. In the morning you get what changed, not a blank ticket.
